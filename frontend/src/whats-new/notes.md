## 1.14.0

- Transactions are grouped by day, and you can show only money in or only money out.
- Bank and broker logos now show next to your transactions.
- Dashboard, Accounts, Budgets, Subscriptions, Alerts and Events are simpler and calmer.
- Finance Sentry installs on your phone's home screen and fits the screen.
- You sign in with your lifekit account.
- A bank connection that has expired is now marked as expired.
- You can invite people from the People page in Settings. (owner)
