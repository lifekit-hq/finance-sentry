Who runs this

One person (the owner) runs this copy of Finance Sentry for themselves and the few people they invite. This notice covers this copy only. Finance Sentry is open-source software, and anyone who runs their own copy is responsible for their own.

What it holds

  • Your sign-in email and name, and your settings.
  • The accounts you connect and what they report: balances, transactions, holdings and trades from your banks, exchanges and broker.
  • What you add yourself: budgets, alerts, watchlist, notes and decisions.
  • Your conversations with the AI assistant, if you were given access to it.
  • The income you declare in Settings: for each source its name, amount, currency, how often it is paid, whether it is before or after tax, and its dates. The name and amount are stored encrypted.
  • An access record for the personal details you declare in the app, such as income or goals: when they were changed, or read by a connected client or the AI assistant, and which fields — never the values themselves.
  • The keys that let the app read your connected accounts. They are stored encrypted, and are never shown back to you or included in your data download.

Who else handles it

Only as far as needed to run the app: the server host; the sign-in provider (Logto); the banks, exchanges and broker you choose to connect, through their data providers (TrueLayer, Monobank, Binance, Revolut X, Interactive Brokers); encrypted backups in object storage (Cloudflare R2); and Anthropic, whose AI model receives the parts of your data a question needs when you use the AI assistant. Your data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Cookies

Only the ones the app needs to work: your signed-in session and the state of a bank connection while you set it up. There is no analytics and no advertising, so there is no cookie banner.

How long it is kept

For as long as your account is open. When you close your account, your sign-in and the stored connection keys are removed at once and the rest is kept for a grace period (30 days at the time of writing) so the owner can reopen it if you change your mind. Then everything is erased. Some old technical records are deleted on a schedule (the access record after about 13 months), and encrypted backups age out on theirs.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Settings, then Your data. It is one file with everything this instance holds about you.
  • Close your account: Settings, then the danger zone at the bottom.
  • To correct something, or to ask anything else about your data, contact the owner at the address above. If you are not happy with the answer, you can complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, the owner will tell the people with access.
