The metric is recurrence per class and rounds per run, never the raw findings count. Owner classes are a mechanical first cut for a human to read; every mechanism needs Denys's approval.

Findings by owner class

Disposition: worker or fixer own the defect. Gate error: reviewer no-op, declined, or shipped unfixed. Decomposition: the brief or spec left the choice open. Harness load: the rule lives in a doc that is linked, not auto-loaded.

Recurrence per defect class

Recurring = seen in at least two runs of the window; those are the candidates for a mechanism. Baseline column is findings over 95 runs (not comparable as a rate until the window is 20 runs).
